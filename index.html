Not your usual whodunit

In most mystery games you cross suspects off. Here, every witness might be wrong. You gather noisy evidence that gets more reliable each round, trade it with rival detectives, and decide when you're sure enough to act.

What you need

One phone or tablet, passed around the table. That's it.

Short a detective or two? Fill any seat with an AI detective, play solo against them, or watch an all-AI case unfold.

Optional: printed data sheets, the suspect lineup and location cards, plus one die per player if you'd rather pick destinations with dice.
