InterrogationQuick Reference

Each turn

  1. Donut calls: from the Lead Detective, clockwise, anyone may announce the Donut Shop.
  2. Secret destinations: everyone else picks 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 in secret, then all reveal.
  3. Action cards: one per detective, after destinations are revealed.
  4. Resolve locations in order, 1 through 6.

Turns per round: 3 players: 6 · 4 players: 4 · 5–6 players: 3. The Lead Detective passes left each round.

Locations

Witness reliability

Crime lab: exact 17% · within an hour 44% · never 6+ hours off (the clock runs midnight to midnight). The killer struck 1–16 hours after signing out at the gate, usually 8–9. A crime at the sign-out hour or in the 7 hours before it clears the defendant.

Trial

  1. Prosecutors state their evidence and the hour of the crime.
  2. The app reveals the defendant's alibi: the hour he or she signed out.
  3. Everyone else votes Guilty or Not guilty in secret.
  4. Check the suspect. Guilty ends the game; innocent clears the suspect.

Action cards

Weighing evidence

Each mark counts its round number. Add a suspect's three attribute totals; highest is most likely. Worth it when you're this sure: press release 33% · prosecute 44% · vote Guilty 44%.
